## 1. What KAHF is

KAHF is software for sending messages and files over amateur radio. It works with ordinary radios and software-defined radios.

## 2. Your licence

You must hold the amateur radio licence or permit that your country requires for the station you run. For an automatic station, such as relay mode, you must hold the licence or permit that your country requires for it. In some countries, including Finland, an automatic station needs a licence or permit. You are responsible for knowing and following the rules where you are.

## 3. Relay mode

Relay mode forwards messages while the operator may not be at the station. You must confirm your licence each time you start relay mode. You are responsible for anything your station transmits while relay mode is running, including when you are away.

## 4. Other people's messages

KAHF can relay messages for other stations. You must follow your national rules on relaying other people's traffic. Do not relay messages your country does not allow you to carry.

## 5. Internet relay

When internet relay is on, KAHF may send a message over the internet if it cannot reach the station on air. Nothing is sent over the internet until the gateway is available and you send a message that way.

## 6. Transmitting

You are responsible for what your station sends and for keeping to the band plan and the power rules in your country. KAHF warns you when a frequency is outside the amateur segments, but you decide what to transmit.
