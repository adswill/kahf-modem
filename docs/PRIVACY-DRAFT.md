# KAHF privacy notice (DRAFT)

Draft for lawyer review. Not legal advice. Do not publish until a lawyer has checked it for the countries where KAHF is used.

## 1. What is shared on the public map

If you keep the default Precise setting, your exact position is shown on the public map at kahf.dev. You can choose Area, which shows a circle without coordinates, or Off, which shows nothing. You can change this at any time in Settings.

## 2. What the server stores

The server keeps your callsign, your role (station or relay), your position if you chose to share it, when your station was last seen, and relay counts. Records expire 48 hours after they were last refreshed.

## 3. What stays on your computer

Your messages, files, contacts, logbook and station key are stored on your computer. The station key is generated on your computer and never leaves it.

## 4. Messages on air

Messages sent over radio are not encrypted. Anyone who can receive the signal can read them. Signed messages prove who sent them but do not hide their content.

## 5. Your choices

You can turn off location sharing, relay mode and internet relay at any time. Stopping the app stops relay mode.
